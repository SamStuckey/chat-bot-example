# Referral program: frequently asked questions

**How do I refer a friend?**
1. Open **Account** and tap **Invite Friends**.
2. Copy your referral code, or tap **Share link** to send it in a message.
3. Your friend enters the code when they create their Greenpenny account.

**Where do I find my referral code?**
It's at the top of the **Invite Friends** page. Penny can also look it up for
you.

**What do we get?**
When the referral is complete, you and your friend each earn 500 bonus
Pennies for your own schools.

**When is a referral complete?**
When your friend has linked a school and scanned their first receipt with a
participating product, within 30 days of creating their account. The bonus
arrives within 48 hours after that.

**My friend forgot to enter my code. Can they still add it?**
Yes, within 7 days of creating their account: they go to **Account > Enter a
referral code**. After 7 days it can't be added.

**My friend already had a Greenpenny account. Does it count?**
No. Referrals are only for people new to Greenpenny, including people who
deleted an earlier account.

**How do I check on a referral?**
The **Invite Friends** page lists everyone who used your code, with a status
for each: *Joined* (account created), *Almost there* (school linked or first
receipt scanned, but not both yet) or *Complete*.

**My referral says Complete, but I don't see the bonus.**
Bonus Pennies can take up to 48 hours to appear. If it's been longer than
that, contact Greenpenny support under **Account > Help & Support**.

**How many friends can I refer?**
As many as you like. Bonuses are paid for up to 20 completed referrals per
calendar year.
