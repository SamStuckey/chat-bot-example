# How the Greenpenny app works

## Pennies
Every participating product a user buys earns Pennies for the school linked
to their account. 100 Pennies is one dollar for the school. Greenpenny pays
each school what its families earned at the end of every month.

## Linking a school
Users pick the school they support under **Account > My School**. An account
supports one school at a time. Users can switch schools once every 30 days;
Pennies already earned stay with the school they were earned for.

## Scanning receipts
Users record purchases by scanning their receipt on the **Scan** tab within
14 days of the purchase. Only participating products earn Pennies. Pennies
show up within 48 hours of the receipt being processed. A blurry or cut-off
receipt may be rejected; the app explains why and asks for a new photo.

## Bonus Pennies
Bonus Pennies are limited-time offers that add extra Pennies on specific
products. There's nothing to clip or activate: a scanned receipt that includes
the product during the offer's dates earns the bonus automatically. Current
offers are on the **Offers** tab, and Penny can show the active ones as cards.

## A school's progress
The **School** tab shows how many Pennies the user's school has earned this
month and how many families are supporting it.

## Getting help from support
For anything Penny can't help with, such as account problems, a missing
bonus, or a receipt that won't scan, users can reach Greenpenny support under
**Account > Help & Support > Contact us**. Support replies within one business
day.
