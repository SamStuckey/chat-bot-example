# Referral program rules

## Who can take part
- Any Greenpenny user with a linked school can refer friends.
- The friend must be new to Greenpenny: no earlier account with the same
  email address or phone number.
- Both people must live in the United States and be at least 18.

## How a referral works
1. The user shares their referral code or link from **Account > Invite
   Friends**.
2. The friend enters the code while creating their account. If they forget,
   they can add it within 7 days under **Account > Enter a referral code**.
   After 7 days a code can't be added.
3. The referral is complete when the friend links a school and scans their
   first receipt with a participating product, within 30 days of creating
   their account.

## The bonus
- When a referral is complete, both people earn 500 bonus Pennies for their
  own linked schools.
- Bonus Pennies are added within 48 hours of the referral completing.
- Each user can earn referral bonuses for up to 20 completed referrals per
  calendar year. Referrals beyond that still count on the Invite Friends
  page, but don't earn a bonus.

## Fair play
- Referring yourself, or creating extra accounts, isn't allowed.
- Codes can't be bought, sold, or posted in spam.
- Greenpenny can withhold bonuses or close accounts that break these rules.
- Greenpenny can change the program with 30 days' notice in the app.
